1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how notaxovertimecalc.com ("the site," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our site. NoTaxOvertimeCalc is a free calculator and educational resource for the federal No Tax on Overtime deduction created by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act.

We've written this policy in plain English. If anything is unclear, contact us and we'll explain.

2. Information We Collect

2a. Information you give us voluntarily

The only personal data we receive directly is what you type into our contact form:

  • Name — so we can address our reply
  • Email address — so we can reply
  • Message content — so we know what you're asking about

We use this only to reply to your inquiry. We keep it until the matter is resolved, up to a maximum of 12 months. We do not add you to any mailing list, send you newsletters, or share your contact details with anyone else.

2b. Information collected automatically (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site. GA4 collects:

  • Pages you visit and how long you spend on them
  • Approximate geographic location (country and region — not your street address)
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • How you arrived at the site (search engine, direct visit, referral)
  • Scroll depth and basic click interactions

GA4 does not collect your name, email address, income figures, or anything you enter into the calculator. The data is aggregated — we see "X visitors used the calculator this week," not individual user actions tied to identities.

Legal basis under GDPR: Legitimate interests (analytics) for understanding traffic patterns; Consent for any advertising-related cookies.

2c. Calculator inputs — important

Your calculator data never leaves your browser Important

All calculations performed by this tool happen entirely within your browser. No data you enter into the calculator — your hourly rate, income, overtime hours, filing status, MAGI — is transmitted to our servers or stored anywhere on our end. We have no access to, and no way to retrieve, your financial inputs. The site is static HTML; there is no database to write to.

If you use the "Share Result" feature, your inputs are encoded into a URL that you control. Sharing that URL is your choice.

5. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited data we receive to:

  • Reply to messages you send through the contact form
  • Understand site traffic patterns so we can improve content (Google Analytics)
  • Display relevant advertising (Google AdSense)

We do not:

  • Sell your data to anyone
  • Share your data with third parties except as described in Section 6
  • Send you marketing or promotional emails
  • Build advertising profiles ourselves
  • Use your data for any purpose not described in this policy

6. Data Sharing

We share data only with the following parties, only for the stated purposes:

  • Google LLC — for Google Analytics (traffic measurement) and Google AdSense (advertising). See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree, Inc. — processes contact form submissions on our behalf and forwards them to us via email. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — provides website hosting and basic security/anti-abuse protection. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.
  • Legal authorities — only if required by law (e.g., a valid court order or subpoena).

We do not sell personal data. Ever.

7. Your Rights

If you are in the EU, EEA, or UK (GDPR rights)

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection — object to our processing of your data
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, where consent is the legal basis

If you are in California (CCPA rights)

  • Right to know — what personal data we collect and how we use it (this policy explains both)
  • Right to delete — request deletion of personal information we hold about you
  • Right to opt out of sale — we do not sell personal data, so there's nothing to opt out of
  • Right to non-discrimination — we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights

How to exercise your rights

Email us at contact@notaxovertimecalc.com with your request. We respond within 30 days as required by GDPR. We may need to verify your identity before processing the request.

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data through our contact form, contact us at contact@notaxovertimecalc.com and we will delete it immediately.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or accuracy of those sites. Review each external site's privacy policy independently before sharing any personal data with them.

Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For material changes, we'll add a clearly visible notice at the top of this page for at least 30 days.
